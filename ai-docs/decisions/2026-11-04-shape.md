# Decisions, 2026-11-04

- **One skill plus CLI.** Design, generate, check and finish are steps of one workflow. Split only if evals show a trigger problem.
- **Singing.** voicewright keeps identity; musicwright generates songs. No singing route: the research has no independent comparison of singing engines.
- **Bible.** Extend cinewright's file. Its schema now accepts the new fields and both forms of `rights`.
- **Speaker model.** WeSpeaker CAM++ ONNX, 27 MB, optional. Small enough not to count as an engine install; fetched with `vw.py setup`.
- **No denoiser by default.** Research found no direct test; the lab has one measurement and no listen.
