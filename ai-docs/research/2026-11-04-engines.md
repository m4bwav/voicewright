# Voice engines for voicewright (2026-11-04)

Read this before choosing voicewright's default engines.

**Marks.** H = I read the primary source (licence, model card, README, docs, paper or API). M = a named test or a reputable secondary source. L = a blog, an aggregator, or one anecdote. U = unverified.
**Sources.** [S1] and the like point to the table at the end. Each source carries its check date. All were checked 2026-11-04.
**Hardware.** PC = 16 GB RTX 5060 Ti. Mac = Apple Silicon, 32 GB. "Fits" is my reading of published sizes unless marked as a report. Nothing was installed or run.

## How weak the evidence is

- **Nobody here listened.** I cannot judge audio. Every quality rank rests on votes, papers or other people's reports. Listen before locking a default.
- **Reddit is nearly absent.** Direct Reddit search is refused. The only Reddit content is three comments quoted in one review [S8]. Say so if a pick rests on community opinion.
- **No YouTube test was found.** The one search aimed at it returned only an auto-generated comparison site [S39]. Treat this job as not done.
- **TTS Arena V2 could not be read.** The Space shows no table to a fetch [S2]. One blog quotes Chatterbox at 1481 +/- 19 on 2026-10-10 [S5] (L). Another site withdrew its Arena numbers on 2026-09-30 because it could not verify them [S4].
- **The Artificial Analysis board tests each provider's own preset voices [S1].** It does not test cloning or local quantized runs. A low rank does not settle a clone job. Qwen3 TTS at 929 is the hosted model, not the design-then-clone recipe.
- **Most listicles are unmeasured.** OpenVox says it relies on documentation [S6]. Yaps ran no listening test [S5]. Only one small panel was found, six listeners in April, and it left out the newest models [S7].

## Ranked answers

"Commercial" below means a small creator can sell the output under the licence as read. See the licence table for limits.

### 1. Voice design from text

| Rank | Engine | Why | Sure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hosted 1 | ElevenLabs Voice Design, used with v3 first | Description of 20-1000 characters, 3 previews. Docs warn designed voices may be less performative on v4 than on earlier models [S11, S12]. AA ranks v4 top on preset voices, which does not cover designed ones [S1] | H for docs, U for quality |
| Local 1 | VoxCPM2 | Apache-2.0, 48 kHz, voice design and cloning in one model, about 8 GB [S13]. One hands-on tester preferred it to Qwen3-TTS for cloning, not design [S14] | M |
| Local 2 | Qwen3-TTS | Documented "Voice Design then Clone" recipe, Apache-2.0 [S15]. Best timbre consistency in the one scene-level paper [S9] | H for recipe |
| Local 3 | MOSS-VoiceGenerator | Apache-2.0, description plus text in the prompt [S16]. No reported results found | L |
| Avoid | OmniVoice design | Has design, but weights are CC-BY-NC [S17] | H |

Inference: design once, render a clean reference clip, then always clone from that frozen clip. A design prompt alone is not a stable identity. Only Qwen3 documents this end to end [S15].

### 4. Acted emotion

| Rank | Engine | Why | Sure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hosted 1 | Eleven v4 with audio tags | Docs say tags are "not perfect yet" [S11]. Stability trades range for consistency | H for docs |
| Hosted 2 | Fish Audio Drama 3 API | Elo 1272, sixth, built for multi-character scenes [S1, S27]. $10 per 1M characters until 2026-11-12, list $50 [S10] | M |
| Local 1 | IndexTTS-2.5 | 8-value emotion vector, reference clip or text [S28]. IndexTTS2 acted best in the paper (UAR 81.1%) but held timbre only third-best (SCS 0.74) [S9]. That paper tested 2, not 2.5. So: check likeness per take | H for paper |
| Local 2 | DramaBox | Stage directions in the prompt, 10 s optional reference, English [S30]. Peak about 24 GB on the card. One test saw about 16 GB [S31]. PC is borderline, low-memory modes reported [S31] | M/L |
| Local 3 | Higgs TTS 3 4B | Inline tags, 100+ languages per TTS Audio Suite [S29]. Creator grant needs a credit [S33]. Elo only 1037 on preset voices [S1] | M |

The paper's key warning: all four tested systems under-acted on at least 81% of lines, and no system won all measures [S9]. Plan for several takes.

### 8. Singing

The weakest area. I found no independent listening comparison.

| Rank | Engine | Why | Sure |
|---|---|---|---|
| New vocal from a clip | SoulX-Singer | Apache-2.0. Zero-shot, melody or MIDI control. Mandarin, English, Cantonese training data [S44]. VRAM U | H for terms, U for quality |
| Change a sung take | SoulX-Singer-SVC | Same repo, no transcription needed [S44] | H for terms, U for quality |
| Change a sung take, trained | RVC | MIT, 100-300 MB, per-voice training, huge community [S29, S43] | M |
| Change a sung take, zero-shot | Seed-VC | Own evaluation: better similarity, slightly lower DNSMOS than RVCv2 [S45]. GPL-3.0 and the repo is now archived [S43] | M, maker's test |
| Score-accurate control | DiffSinger with OpenUtau | Apache-2.0, active, pushed 2026-11-03 [S43, S46]. Needs a score and a voicebank. Licence of each voicebank must be checked | M |
| Whole song | ACE-Step 1.5 XL | MIT code, vocals inside a generated song [S43, S47]. Not a persistent singer. English vocals reported as strong, Japanese weaker, one anecdote [S47]. Route this to musicwright | L |

Inference for a stable singer: generate a song, separate the vocal, then convert it to the character with SoulX-Singer-SVC or RVC.

## Corrections to the 2026-10-07 note

- **OmniVoice** is not simply Apache-2.0. The weights are CC-BY-NC (H) [S17].
- **IndexTTS** is usable commercially below the thresholds, against what Yaps says about contacting them (H) [S28, S5]. Read the whole licence before shipping.
- **TTS Audio Suite** LICENSE file is MIT (H), v5.10.7, now 19 engines with DramaBox, OmniVoice, MOSS and Fish S2 Pro [S29]. Each engine keeps its own licence. Drama 3 is not in its table.
- **Fish:** code and Drama 3 moved to a Community licence on 2026-10-13. S2 Pro stays research only (H) [S27].
- **Seed-VC** is archived (H) [S43]. **Chatterbox** README now says it is an archive with no support, though the repo still receives pushes (H) [S18, S43].
- **VibeVoice:** the note was right to distrust it. Its TTS demo is still marked disabled (H) [S54].
- **New since the note:** Drama 3, DramaBox, Breeze TTS 2, Ming-3.0, Audio8 Realistic Air, Phonon, Qwen-Audio-3.1 (hosted), Voxtral 3 (hosted), Sonic 3.6 [S1].

## Open-weights board

Artificial Analysis, preset voices only [S1] (H, page has no data date): Drama 3 1272, Drama 3 Preview 1239, Breeze TTS 2 1226, Ming-3.0 1189, Phonon 31M 1111, S2 Pro 1108, Voxtral 1089, Kokoro 1061, Higgs 1037, Chatterbox 1022, VibeVoice 953. Top overall: Eleven v4 1334, v4 Turbo 1331, Qwen-Audio-3.1 Plus 1301, Sonic 3.6 1293, Gemini 3.8 Flash 1282.

So the three best open models on this board cannot be used commercially at scale, or will not fit the PC. Drama 3 needs too much memory. Breeze is non-commercial. Ming-3.0 is the one to test (MIT, 0.5B). This is my reading.

**The gap, my inference.** I found no plugin that treats a voice as a lasting asset. Needed: a voice sheet, design then freeze, a licence gate before generation, an engine router by job and hardware, a likeness check per line against the character's own baseline, a take picker, and a consent and disclosure record. Search coverage was shallow, so this is M at best.

## Suggested first tests for voicewright

1. Listen to the tts-audiobook-tool samples [S26].
2. On the PC, render the same 10 lines with VoxCPM2, Qwen3-TTS 1.7B, Ming-3.0 0.5B, IndexTTS-2.5 and Audio8 Realistic Air. Record VRAM, RTF and likeness. No 5060 Ti figures exist for any of them.
3. Check that the Mac runs the same set through mlx-audio.
4. Test SoulX-Singer VRAM and whether a sung result holds the same identity as the speech voice.
